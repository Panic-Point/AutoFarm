    Commands

    Each action takes one tick. Crops grow every tick. Looking around is free. Words with a lock open in a later chapter.

    Actions

    • move(NORTH) SOUTH EAST WEST. Returns False at the edge.
    • harvest() collects a ripe crop. An unripe crop is lost.
    • till() turns grass into soil, or soil back into grass.
    • plant(WHEAT) on soil. CARROT costs 2 wheat, LETTUCE 2 hay, STRAWBERRY 3 wheat, BLUEBERRY 2 carrots, PUMPKIN 1 carrot, CORN 1 wheat and SUNFLOWER 2 wheat.
    • wait() does nothing for one tick.
    • water() wets the soil for 6 ticks and uses 1 drop from the can. refill() fills the can (it holds 5) when Bolt is on the well.

    Looking

    • is_ripe() is True when the crop under Bolt is ready.
    • is_rotten() is True when the pumpkin under Bolt has rotted.
    • crop_here() and ground_here() say what's under Bolt: crop_here() == CARROT, ground_here() == SOIL.
    • can_move(EAST) is True if Bolt can step that way.
    • position() gives (x, y), with (0, 0) in the south-west corner. field_size() is how wide the field is.
    • count(WHEAT) says how many Bolt has, and count("hay") how much hay. ticks() counts ticks used.
    • is_dry() is True when the soil under Bolt is dry. water_left() says how much water is in the can.
    • ripe_at(x, y) is True if there's a ripe sunflower at (x, y). Sunflowers are tall, so Bolt can see them from anywhere.
    • print(...) writes to the console below your code.

    Loops and choices

    • repeat 5 times: runs the indented lines below it that many times. In Python it's for i in range(5):
    • A repeat inside a repeat does a whole loop each time round.
    • if ...: and else: choose what to do. Compare with == (same), != (not the same), <, >, <= and >=.
    • else if ...: (or elif ...:) asks another question when the first answer was no.
    • repeat until is_ripe(): keeps going until something is true. repeat while ...: keeps going while it is true. repeat forever: never stops on its own, and break jumps out. In Python: while not is_ripe():, while ...: and while True:
    • set total to 0 and change total by 1 make and update variables (total = 0 and total += 1 in Python). for x from 1 to 10: counts, like for x in range(1, 11):
    • and, or and not join questions: if is_dry() and not is_ripe():
    • define sweep_row: (or def sweep_row():) makes your own command. Run it with sweep_row().
    • give back total (or return total) ends your command and hands back an answer: set n to ripe_in_row(2).
    • [WHEAT, CARROT] is a list. plan[0] is its first crop and len(plan) how many it has. for each crop in plan: (or for crop in plan:) goes through it. At the festival, design() gives a list of rows to plant.
    • add crop to basket (or basket.append(crop)) puts something on the end of a list.
    • On market days, order() gives the list of crops Mayor Maple wants, one of each. crop in basket checks whether a list has something in it.
    • Lines inside a loop, choice or command are indented by 4 spaces.

    Code windows

    • Split your code into windows. Make one with + New window, and double-click a window's name to rename it.
    • use helper lets a window use every command made in the window called helper. use go_home from helper takes just one. In Python: from helper import *
    • Each window has its own ▶ button. Run at the top runs the window you used last.
    • Drag a window by its title bar, and resize it from its bottom-right corner.

    Crops

    • Grass: grows back by itself, ripe after 4 ticks, gives hay.
    • Wheat: ripe after 8 ticks, gives wheat.
    • Carrot: costs 2 wheat, ripe after 14 ticks, gives a carrot. Plant it where wheat was just picked and it gives 2!
    • Lettuce: costs 2 hay, ripe after 6 ticks, gives a lettuce. It wilts 8 ticks after it's ripe, so pick the first one you planted first!
    • Strawberry: costs 3 wheat, ripe after 12 ticks. Gives 1 strawberry, plus 1 more for each strawberry right next to it when it gets ripe.
    • Blueberry: costs 2 carrots, ripe after 24 ticks. The bush stays when you pick it and is ripe again 10 ticks later. It gives 2 blueberries, then 1 more each time, up to 5. Picking it early loses the whole bush.
    • Pumpkin: costs 1 carrot, ripe after 16 ticks. It rots 30 ticks after that. A rotten pumpkin can't be picked, but till() clears it away.
    • Mushroom: grows by itself in the fog, and can't be planted.
    • Corn: costs 1 wheat, and only grows while its soil is wet. Ripe after 10 wet ticks, gives 2 corn.
    • Sunflower: costs 2 wheat, ripe after 40 ticks. So tall Bolt can see it from anywhere.
