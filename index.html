AutoFarm

Write Python. Run the farm.

Tick 0

Commands

Each action takes one tick. Crops grow every tick. Looking around is free.

Actions

  • move(NORTH) SOUTH EAST WEST. Returns False at the edge.
  • harvest() collects a ripe crop. An unripe crop is lost.
  • till() turns grass into soil, or soil back into grass.
  • plant(Crop.WHEAT) on soil. Crop.CARROT costs 2 wheat.
  • wait() does nothing for one tick.

Looking

  • is_ripe(), crop_here(), ground_here()
  • position() gives (x, y), with (0, 0) in the south-west corner.
  • field_size(), count("wheat"), ticks()
  • print(...) writes to the console below your code.

Crops

  • Grass: grows back by itself, ripe after 4 ticks, gives hay.
  • Wheat: ripe after 8 ticks, gives wheat.
  • Carrot: ripe after 14 ticks, gives a carrot.
